| 1 · Introduction | Scouty is an AI sales associate for SMEs. We want to make consistent B2B prospecting accessible to small teams, with a practical prospecting workflow for Australian service businesses. |
| 2 · Problem | Serving current customers leaves little time to find the next ones. Research, outreach and follow-ups compete with delivery when the owner or a lean team handles sales. |
| 3 · Hiring commitment | For an SME with a limited hiring budget, a dedicated sales hire is a significant recurring commitment. Jobs and Skills Australia reports median full-time sales representative earnings of A$1,692 per week, using ABS May 2025 data. Annualised and rounded, that is about A$88,000, not total hiring cost. The owner needs new opportunities before being ready to commit to another salary. |
| 4 · Customer | Our initial customers are service SMEs with an established offer, paying customers and business buyers reached through calls and quotes. For this Australian pitch, we are seeking pilots in technical services and corporate training. This is a proposed local validation effort, not evidence of Australian traction. |
| 5 · Scouty | The business website gives Scouty the starting context. It helps find prospects, prepare outreach for review and coordinate interested conversations. The business stays in control and closes the sale. This is practical assistance with a bounded task, not a claim to replace an entire employee. |
| 6 · Early signal | After one week of development, we have two B2B partners: ATD at UEH and TechnicAir Singapore Services. These are founder-reported relationships. The proposed next step is to agree one use case per partner and measure repeat value; no completed pilot outcomes are claimed. |
| 7 · Model | Start with $30 for 1,000 emails with follow-ups. Pay as you go for more. The proposition is consistent prospecting without committing to another salary.
