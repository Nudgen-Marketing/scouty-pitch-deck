## Segmentation decision

Owner-confirmed: B2B service SMEs in Vietnam / Southeast Asia, then expand.

Proposed operational ICP:

- Offer: expertise-led B2B service sold through a meeting, consultation or quote.
- Buyer: owner, business-development lead or small sales team.
- Constraint: no dedicated prospecting capacity.
- Trigger: a new service, a new target audience or a growth objective requiring more buyer conversations.
- Candidate segments: corporate training, technical services, specialist consulting.
- Product-fit qualification: identifiable business buyers, usable company context and willingness to use an email-led workflow. Channel preference, language coverage and contact quality require pilot checks.

Consumer retail, large enterprise sales operations and businesses whose buying process does not fit the workflow should not be counted automatically. The ATD and TechnicAir relationships provide contexts to explore, not proof of ICP fit, recurring usage or payment.

## Sizing still required

No unsupported numeric SAM/SOM is included. Build it from eligible business counts by country and sector, qualified workflow fit and validated annual spend. Count legal businesses consistently; do not combine establishments, informal household businesses and firms without explaining definitions. Use a separate acquisition-capacity model for near-term obtainable accounts.

The market slide deliberately presents category scale and the narrower entry separately. It does not multiply all SMEs by an invented price, assume a percentage of global category spend, or label all AI-using SMEs potential paying Scouty users.
