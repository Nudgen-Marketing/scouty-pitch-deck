# Scouty pitch narrative

Updated 11 October 2026. Audience: Vietnam entrepreneurship program. Owner-approved thesis: **Small service businesses need consistent prospecting without committing to another salary.**

## Eight-slide sequence

1. An AI sales associate for SMEs: accessible prospecting, starting with Vietnamese service firms.
2. Serving today's customers leaves little time to find tomorrow's: research, outreach and follow-ups compete with delivery.
3. A dedicated hire is a major commitment: retain the sourced monthly salary benchmark.
4. Focus on established service SMEs, testing technical services and corporate training in Vietnam before regional expansion.
5. A practical, controlled workflow: website context, buyer research, approved outreach and interested conversations.
6. Two reported partners after one week; propose one use case per partner as the next step.
7. $30 for 1,000 emails with follow-ups, with pay-as-you-go for more activity.
8. Ask for SME pilot introductions and evaluation support; measure time, conversations, repeat paid usage and cost to serve.

Initial geography: Vietnam and Southeast Asia. First segments to test: technical services and corporate training. The partner relationships provide learning access, not proof of segment fit.

The owner approved removing the unsupported 1–5 discovery-call range. The deck no longer presents it as a result, target or guarantee. It also removes the 24/7 angle: consistent prospecting and owner capacity are the central problem. The $30 offer comes from the owner; currency, exact billing units and follow-up counting remain unspecified. No per-email rate is inferred. Team details remain to be supplied.

## SME-focused program framing

The owner confirmed that the program focuses on small and medium-sized enterprises. SMEs are now explicit throughout the opening, problem, ICP, solution, pricing and program ask. The target remains the narrower subset of B2B service SMEs with established offers and limited sales capacity. Affordability and time savings are intended benefits to validate, not measured outcomes. No broad economic-impact or employment claim has been added.

## Research-informed program revision

This revision applies practical problem-solving, accessible costs and measurable impact as editorial priorities. It does not assert that historical VIC 2024 criteria govern a current round. VIC 2025 emphasizes ViGen; current eligibility and pitch format remain unverified. The deck adds no program badge, eligibility claim, Vietnamese-language capability claim or invented founder/team credentials.
