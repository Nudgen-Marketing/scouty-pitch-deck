# Evidence and claim boundaries

Updated 11 October 2026.

## Salary benchmark

[CareerViet VietnamSalary — Sales Executive](https://vietnamsalary.careerviet.vn/detail/sales-executive-kw) displays an average of **₫12.6 million per month** in the page read on 11 October 2026, aggregating 564 advertised job samples in that retrieved version. Counts and experience breakdowns vary between search and page versions; only the displayed overall monthly benchmark is used.

This is a job-board salary reference across industries and locations, not a representative wage census, an entry-level-only salary, a minimum wage, or total employer cost. Its sampling period is not stated on the retrieved page. No additional commission, statutory contribution, recruitment or training cost is quantified. The deck does not claim this is a fresh 2026 wage survey.

## Product

[Scouty](https://scouty.to/) publicly describes company research, company/contact discovery, editable outreach, reply tracking and Google Calendar booking. These public descriptions were read on 11 October. They are not independently verified customer outcomes. Human approval remains in the story.

One person's limited working hours do not establish Scouty uptime, unattended operation, round-the-clock sending or a 24/7 service guarantee. No such claim is made.

## Owner-confirmed facts

- Audience: entrepreneurship program in Vietnam.
- Initial target: B2B service SMEs in Vietnam/Southeast Asia, then expand.
- Two B2B partners after one week of development; no other traction supplied.
- Business model: **pay-as-you-go for email and related usage**, not workspace subscription. This supersedes the previous subscription hypothesis. Rates and billable-unit definitions remain unspecified; paid usage and billing implementation were not verified.

The partners are [ATD at UEH](https://atd.ueh.edu.vn/) and [TechnicAir Singapore Services](https://www.technicairglobal.com/). Their sites establish organizational identities and service descriptions, not independent verification of Scouty relationships. No paid contracts, completed pilots, active usage, outcomes or endorsement quotes are claimed.

## Hypotheses and gaps

Owner-led firms unable to justify a dedicated hire are the target segment; this is not a claim that all SMEs reject sales hiring. Training, technical services and consulting are candidate segments. Need interviews, partner scope, usage, repeat value, willingness to pay, cost to serve, and team credentials. Program support requests remain proposed.

The market-sizing and AI-adoption slides and go-to-market slide were removed at the owner's request. Earlier market research is archived separately and is not part of the current pitch.

## Owner-approved commercial offer and framing

The owner approved “$30 for 1,000 emails with follow-ups. Pay as you go for more.” This is owner-supplied commercial positioning, not a verified billing implementation. Currency, follow-up counting and exact billable units remain unspecified. No per-email rate is inferred.

The previously suggested 1–5 discovery-call range has been removed entirely. It is not supported as a measured result. No call conversion or guarantee appears in the deck.

The current problem is interrupted prospecting when the owner is busy delivering work, plus the commitment of hiring. The 24/7 comparison was removed. The refined ICP is an owner-led service business with an established offer, paying customers, identifiable B2B buyers and sales through calls and quotes, without consistent prospecting capacity. Technical services and corporate training are initial segments to test, not validated winners.

## SME program alignment

The owner confirmed the program is SME-focused. The current deck foregrounds limited team capacity and hiring budgets. It does not broaden the ICP to all SMEs: the target remains established B2B service businesses. Time saved, affordability and consistent pipeline are intended benefits/pilot measures, not independently verified outcomes.
