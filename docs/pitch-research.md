# Scouty: pitch research and recommended revision

Research date: 11 October 2026. Scope: a representative comparison of Gstack, YC, Sequoia and several prominent competitions, not an exhaustive survey of every competition. The exact Vietnam program and pitch duration remain unknown; its published rubric should take precedence once provided. This is a research recommendation; it does not replace the current presentation.

## Main finding

The current SME framing is clearer, but the deck still needs evidence of the customer's actual workflow, a convincing reason to choose Scouty and a founder/team story. More market statistics and more feature slides would not resolve these weaknesses.

Recommended thesis: **Scouty helps B2B service SMEs keep prospecting while their small teams run the business.**

Keep “An AI sales associate for SMEs” as the memorable cover, then explain its bounded job immediately: find business buyers, prepare outreach and follow up, with business approval. Avoid suggesting that $30 replaces an employee's full responsibilities.

## What the sources contribute

### Gstack — customer diagnosis before slide polish

[Repository](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack) · [Startup diagnostic](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/main/office-hours/sections/phase-2a-startup-diagnostic.md)

The material reviewed is a startup diagnostic within a development toolkit, not a competition's official pitch rubric. It examines actual demand, existing workarounds, a specific person with a specific need, the smallest paid use case, observed behaviour and future relevance. It prioritizes customer behaviour over stated enthusiasm.

Application: learn who at ATD or TechnicAir owns prospecting, how they do it today, what is interrupted, and what they have agreed to do with Scouty. “SMEs” is an audience category; it is not enough to explain a buying decision. The two relationships should support a learning story only as far as their actual commitments warrant.

### Y Combinator — clarity and a few memorable reasons to believe

[How to Pitch Your Company](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/how-to-pitch-your-company/) · [Demo Day guide](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches)

YC recommends a plain explanation of the company, succinct answers about the business, progress and a direct ask. For Scouty, the audience should remember a specific SME problem, an accessible usage-based offer and credible early execution. Keep one clear claim per slide and rehearse for comprehension, not merely to hit a time limit. A listener should be able to explain the business back without help.

### Hult Prize — business and impact alongside the idea and team

[Selection criteria](https://www.hultprize.org/how-it-works/selection-process-criteria)

Hult's public framework evaluates team, idea, impact and business, adding further criteria at later stages. This is useful as an impact-oriented comparison, not proof that the unnamed Vietnam program uses Hult's rubric. Scouty's relevance should be concrete: smaller firms can test prospecting with limited budgets. Measure owner time, relevant conversations and repeat use. Do not invent employment, revenue or SDG impact.

### Rice Business Plan Competition — assess the business behind the presentation

[Judging guidance](https://rbpc.rice.edu/judges)

Rice describes judging in terms of investment potential and asks founders to explain competitive advantage and what support enables. For Scouty, prepare the economics of the $30 offer and a specific next milestone. Its investment-oriented emphasis should not be copied wholesale into an SME support program. The page mixes year labels; this report does not rely on its event dates.

### Startup Battlefield — demonstration and differentiation

[Official overview](https://techcrunch.com/startup-battlefield/about/)

Battlefield emphasizes working demos, differentiation and founder credibility. Scouty needs evidence for each.

### Slush 100 — compress the story

[Official competition page](https://slush.org/audience/startups/slush100)

The page describes a one-minute pitch at one selection stage. Prepare a short version that preserves the customer, problem, approach and strongest proof. Do not assume this is the Vietnam program's format.

### Sequoia — a completeness check

[Writing a Business Plan](https://articles.sequoiacap.com/writing-a-business-plan)

Sequoia covers purpose, problem, solution, timing, customer/market, alternatives, business model, team and vision. Use those topics to check for unanswered questions, not as a requirement for ten separate slides. For Scouty, alternatives and team are the most material omissions. Keep the user-requested exclusion of dedicated market and go-to-market slides; prepare those answers in notes or an appendix if the actual rubric requires them.

## Recommended core sequence

Keep a short core pitch. Add a team slide once facts are supplied; combine customer and problem if the slot requires eight slides.

1. **Scouty: an AI sales associate for SMEs.** One sentence explaining prospecting support.
2. **When client work takes over, finding the next client stops.** Tell one real SME story. Until evidence is available, use general problem wording rather than an invented case.
3. **A dedicated sales hire is a monthly commitment.** Retain the salary benchmark and its scope; explain the cost of adding capacity, not guaranteed savings.
4. **Start with SMEs that already sell expertise.** Paying customers, identifiable business buyers, meetings and quotes, thin prospecting capacity. Choose the first vertical after partner interviews.
5. **Keep prospecting moving in one approved workflow.** One real example of company research → approved outreach → follow-up. Describe outcomes only if observed.
6. **Two early partners after one week.** Specify what “partner” means and what happens next. Use actual commitment as the evidence.
7. **Start with $30. Pay as you go.** Define the 1,000-email offer precisely. Keep unit-cost and margin calculations in backup material.
8. **Why this team.** Verified founder roles and relevant experience. Currently blocked on missing information; do not copy Mermail credentials.
9. **Help us prove repeat value for SMEs.** Proposed pilot plan and concrete program support request, with measurable endpoints.

A proposed pilot ask could be: “Help us recruit a focused cohort of service SMEs and measure whether they save prospecting time, start relevant buyer conversations and return for another paid campaign.” Set cohort size and duration only after agreeing capacity and program requirements.

## What to collect before another major rewrite

1. Exact program link/rubric, pitch duration, Q&A duration and language.
2. One actual buyer at each partner: role, current process, trigger and pain in their own words.
3. What each partner has committed: meeting, pilot agreement, data access, usage, payment or something else.
4. Meaning of the $30 package: currency, total sends versus unique contacts, follow-ups, lead-data inclusion, AI/sending costs, support burden and payment fees.
5. Founder names, responsibilities and relevant track record specific to Scouty.
6. A real product example and the most important observed customer reaction.

## Rehearsal and Q&A

- Deliver the pitch once without slides. Ask a listener to repeat the customer, pain, offer and reason to believe.
- Record a timed run. Cut repeated context before cutting evidence.
- Prepare precise answers: Why not use existing tools? Who chooses and pays? What did the partners actually do? How can the $30 offer sustain the service? What does this program let you prove?
- Distinguish partner relationships, active use, paid use, interested replies and booked calls. They are different signals.
- Measure performance per SME campaign with comparable definitions: owner time, delivery, relevant replies, agreed calls, repeat paid usage and variable costs. No current values are assumed.

## Program identified: Vietnam Innovation Challenge — edition distinction

The owner supplied a [VnEconomy article dated 14 March 2025](https://vneconomy.vn/chuong-trinh-thach-thuc-doi-moi-sang-tao-2025-xay-dung-nen-tang-ai-vung-chac-tai-viet-nam.htm). It identifies NIC/Meta's Vietnam Innovation Challenge 2025 and its ViGen initiative. Checked against the official site on 11 October 2026.

- [Official VIC 2025 site](https://vic.nic.gov.vn/): focuses on open Vietnamese-language datasets, AI evaluation and tools through ViGen. The reviewed page does not establish a current general SME pitch competition, Scouty's eligibility, or a pitch duration. Do not imply Scouty uses Llama/ViGen or contributes datasets without evidence.
- [Official VIC 2024 page](https://vic.nic.gov.vn/2024-vi): historically sought AI applications improving business productivity and competitiveness. Its criteria cover originality, durable social impact, solving real problems, Vietnam readiness, scalability, organizational capability and cost-effective replication. These are historical criteria, not verified 2025/2026 rules.
- The 2024 page separates startups from an innovative-SME applicant category requiring market deployment and revenue. Selling to SMEs does not itself place Scouty in the SME applicant category. Current category and eligibility need the actual application rules.

Recommended framing remains: practical, affordable AI-assisted prospecting for Vietnamese B2B service SMEs. Demonstrate the workflow, adoption effort, human approval, owner time and repeat usage. Show what partners have actually committed to. Strengthen team evidence and explain sustainable cost to serve. These are tailored editorial recommendations, not claimed official requirements for a current round.

Before altering the deck to claim program alignment, obtain the current invitation/application link, edition, stage and pitch length. The news article is useful context but does not supply these details. Preserve the owner's requested absence of dedicated market/GTM slides.
